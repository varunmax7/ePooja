## Full description

A digital Telugu Smartha purohit for your home.

ePooja computes your local Panchangam — tithi, nakshatram, sunrise, Rahu
kalam and the rest — right on your phone, speaks your Sankalpam correctly
with today's date and your own name and gothram, and guides you step by step
through a puja the way a purohit would: what to do, what to chant, and when.

**Know the day.** Every anga the Panchangam gives — tithi, nakshatram,
yogam, karanam, samvatsaram, ayanam, sunrise and sunset, Rahu kalam,
Yamagandam, Gulika kalam — computed for exactly where you live, on-device,
with no internet connection required.

**Say your Sankalpam correctly.** A personalized, grammatically correct
Sankalpam — spoken and written — with today's Panchangam and your own
details (name, gothram, nakshatram, family) stitched in, for you, your
spouse, or your whole family.

**Prepare with confidence.** A samagri checklist and a naivedyam/prasadam
recipe, scaled to how many you're cooking for, for every puja in the app.

**Be guided through the puja itself.** A step-by-step audio-synced runner
from Poorvangam through the Pradhana Puja to Uttarangam, in Chant mode
(mantras only) or Guided mode (a purohit's spoken instruction before every
step, in Telugu).

**Works in Telugu or English**, with mantras shown in Telugu, Devanagari, or
Roman (IAST) script — whichever you read.

**Built for the diaspora, too.** Your Panchangam and Sankalpam are computed
for wherever you actually live — Hyderabad or Dallas, Vijayawada or London —
not just India.

Nitya Puja plus Ganapathi, Lakshmi and Shiva puja ship today; more deity and
festival pujas are on the way.
